This project will look at whether political alignment is related to COVID-19 spread in Northern Indiana. Counties will be used as the main unit of analysis. The study area will include urban, suburban, and rural counties so that the data represents different types of communities.

Political alignment will be measured using county presidential election results. Instead of simply labeling a county Republican or Democratic, we will use the percentage of votes received by each party. COVID-19 outcomes will be measured using county-level case or death rates, such as cases or deaths per 100,000 people.

The project will also look at whether similar relationships appear in other health or social outcomes. Possible examples include heart disease, diabetes, and firearm-related deaths. These outcomes do not spread in the same way as COVID-19, so the goal is not to treat them as the same type of problem. Instead, we want to see whether similar statistical patterns appear across different outcomes.

Other factors such as population density, age, and income may also be considered because they could affect the results. The goal of the project is to study relationships in the data, not to prove that political alignment causes a disease or health outcome.

Research Question 1:

Is political alignment associated with COVID-19 spread in Northern Indiana?

H₀: ρ = 0- there is no statistically significant relationship between political alignment and COVID-19 outcomes.

Hₐ: ρ ≠ 0- there is a statistically significant relationship between political alignment and COVID-19 outcomes.

We will not assume beforehand whether one political alignment will have higher or lower COVID-19 rates.

Research Question 2:

Does the relationship between political alignment and COVID-19 also appear in other health or social outcomes?

The null hypothesis is that the relationship between political alignment and COVID-19 does not consistently appear in other outcomes.

The alternative hypothesis is that political alignment is also associated with one or more of the other outcomes being studied.

This question will help determine whether any relationship found during COVID-19 appears to be specific to the pandemic or whether similar patterns also appear with outcomes such as, though not limited to, heart disease, diabetes, disinformation, and firearm-related deaths.

This research is important because it may help show whether political alignment is associated with differences in population-level outcomes. If the relationship appears only with COVID-19, factors related specifically to the pandemic may be more important. If similar relationships appear across several different outcomes, broader factors such as population, income, age, or location may help explain the pattern.

The project will be considered successful if we are able to collect reliable data, combine the datasets correctly, and use appropriate statistical methods to answer both research questions. We will use graphs, correlations, and regression models to study the relationships. A significance level of α = 0.05 will be used. A statistically significant result is not required for the project to be successful; finding little or no relationship is also a valid result.

The project will begin by finalizing the research questions, study area, variables, and data sources. We will then collect election data, COVID-19 data, data for the other selected outcomes, and basic county demographic information.

After collecting the data, we will clean and combine the datasets by county. We will check for missing information and calculate comparable rates where needed.

Once the data is prepared, we will create graphs and summary statistics to look for patterns. We will then use correlation and regression models to test the relationships between political alignment and the selected outcomes.

After the analysis is complete, we will compare the COVID-19 results with the other outcomes and consider other factors that may help explain the results. The final stage will include writing the conclusions, creating final graphs, organizing the GitHub repository, checking the analysis, and preparing the final presentation.
